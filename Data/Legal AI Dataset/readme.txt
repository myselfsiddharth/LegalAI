Indian Supreme Court Judgments Archive (1950–2025)

This repository holds an archive of 26,688 Indian Supreme Court judgments from 1950 to 2025. The files are organized into year wise folders and contain Judgement PDF's.

Access Link: 
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1_omgPYIvnrn0WAd9yzkOA-m6iA4EnNX0?usp=sharing

Dataset Structure:

1. Google Drive Folder
   - 26,688 cases. Full-text judgment documents organized by Year (which contains Facts, Issue, Arguments, Courts Reasoning and Judgement for each case)

2. Citations Folder (JSON files)
   - 7,497 cases. Contains citation metadata, including Case Name, Document_ID, Year, and Cited Cases Identifier. The Document IDs match the ones used by Indian Kanoon. You can easily look them up online or plug them into the Indian Kanoon API if you need to get citations for other cases/additional case details.

3. Land Disputes (Excel File)
   - 6,954 cases. A filtered list of cases dealing with immovable property. Columns include Case Name, Year, Document_ID, Indian Kanoon URL and the relative path in the Google Drive. The cases are filtered to only include disputes over land, immovable property, ownership/title, possession, injunctions, partition, boundaries, encroachments, revenue records, sale deeds, inheritance, tenancy/leases, land acquisition, or adverse possession.
